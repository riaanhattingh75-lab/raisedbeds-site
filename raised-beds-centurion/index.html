Raised beds for Centurion gardens

Centurion is one of the areas we regularly deliver to, together with Pretoria and Midrand. Our concrete raised beds are made in Pretoria, and delivery is R350 across Pretoria and Gauteng, Centurion included.

Each bed is built from solid concrete panels that are bolted together, so assembly is easy. Concrete does not rot or rust, and it stands up to sun, rain and frost without any treating or painting.
