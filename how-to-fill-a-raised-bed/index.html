How to fill a raised bed

Level ground, topsoil and compost, plenty of water and a little patience. Here is how to fill a concrete raised bed, and how much soil to order.

The short version

Put the bed on level ground, fill it with a mix of good topsoil and compost, water well, and let it settle before you plant. The rest of this page explains how to do each step and how much soil to get.

Step by step

  1. Choose the spot. Most vegetables and herbs like a sunny position, so pick a place that gets plenty of light. Keep a tap or hose within reach.
  2. Level the ground. Concrete beds are heavy once full, and level ground keeps the panels square and the soil even. Check with a spirit level before you fill.
  3. Assemble the bed. The panels are bolted together, so a few tools and a helper are all you need.
  4. Fill with topsoil and compost. Mix the two rather than layering them, and fill to a little below the top edge to leave room for watering.
  5. Water well. Soak the soil thoroughly. It will sink as air pockets close up.
  6. Let it settle, then top up. Wait for the soil to settle, add more mix if it has dropped, and then plant.

What to grow

At 350mm deep, the beds give enough soil for vegetables, herbs and flowers. In our photos you will find tomatoes growing up trellis mesh, and marigolds with white flowers.
