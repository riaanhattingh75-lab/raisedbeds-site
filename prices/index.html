What a delivered bed costs

Delivery is R350 across Pretoria and Gauteng. For one bed sent to one address, that means the smallest size comes to R1,000 delivered (R650 + R350) and the largest to R2,400 (R2,050 + R350). The Black Friday bundle is R5,000 for the six beds, and delivery is still R350 on top of that. If you are ordering several beds of other sizes, or you are further out, WhatsApp Riaan for a quote rather than adding it up yourself.

Choosing a size

  • 700 × 1000 and 700 × 1200 – the most affordable beds, with a growing area of 0.7 to 0.84 m². Good for herbs, a few tomato plants or a small flower bed.
  • 1000 × 1000 – a square bed of 1 m², easy to reach into from every side.
  • 700 × 1500 – a long, narrow bed that suits a wall, fence or path. Black Friday special: six of these beds, each 1500 mm long × 700 mm wide × 350 mm high, for R5,000 total. Delivery is separate (R350).
  • 1000 × 1500 and 1500 × 1500 – the big beds, for a proper vegetable patch or a feature in a larger garden.

Custom sizes, bulk and branded orders

If none of the six sizes fits your space, custom sizes are available on request. Bulk orders are quoted individually, whether it is for a school, an estate or a nursery. Panels can also carry your name or web address: see branded beds.
