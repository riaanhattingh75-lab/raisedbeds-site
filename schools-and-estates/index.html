Beds that last for schools and estates

Branded Concrete has supplied concrete raised beds for school food gardens and for rows of beds along estate walls and walkways. You can see both in our photo gallery. This page explains why concrete works for this kind of project and how to order.

Why concrete suits a school food garden

  • Built to last – concrete won’t rot or rust, so beds don’t need replacing.
  • Strong – solid panels take the weight of wet soil, and sun, rain and frost don’t bother them.
  • Consistent sizes – there are six standard sizes, so a row of beds looks tidy and every bed has the same depth of 350mm.
  • Easy to assemble – the panels are bolted together.

Why concrete suits estates

Estates tend to want tidy, low-maintenance features. Beds placed in rows along a wall, a fence or a paved walkway give a neat, uniform look, and there is nothing to paint or treat. Panels can also carry your estate’s name: see branded beds.

Planning a bulk order

Bulk orders are available on request, with custom sizes if needed. To get an accurate quote, have these details ready when you message Riaan:

  • How many beds you need, and in which sizes
  • The delivery address or suburb
  • Whether you want branded panels

Standard prices run from R650 for a 700 × 1000mm bed to R2,050 for a 1500 × 1500mm bed, and delivery is R350 across Pretoria and Gauteng. Bulk orders and places further out are quoted individually. The prices page has the full list.

Filling the beds

Each bed is filled with good topsoil and compost, watered well and left to settle before planting. The volume of soil for each size is in our filling guide, which helps you work out how much soil to order for a whole garden.

Where we deliver

We deliver across Gauteng, including Pretoria, Centurion and Midrand, and can confirm Johannesburg on request.
