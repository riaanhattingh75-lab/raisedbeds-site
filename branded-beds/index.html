Your name on the front of the bed

Branded panels are possible on our concrete raised beds, so your name or web address can sit on the front of the bed and work as signage too. It turns a garden bed into a feature with a message, and the concrete means it stays put for years.

What we have made

  • Flower beds carrying the raisedbeds.co.za name – planted with marigolds and white flowers.
  • A bed next to a City of Tshwane bin – branded concrete work for the City of Tshwane.
  • A branded bin for Colbyn Golf Park – made by Branded Concrete.

You can see these in the gallery.

Who branded beds are for

Branded beds are a good fit for estates, schools, businesses and anyone who wants a garden feature with a message. A shop, an office park, a golf club or a school entrance can all use a branded bed to show a name or web address while adding greenery. See schools and estates for how bulk projects work.

How to ask for branded beds

  1. Decide which size you need from the price list. Standard beds run from R650 to R2,050, all 350mm high.
  2. Tell Riaan what you want the panel to say, such as a business name or web address.
  3. Say how many beds you need and where they will go.
  4. He will reply with a quote.

Branded panel pricing is not listed on the site, so ask Riaan for a quote. Delivery is R350 across Pretoria and Gauteng, with bulk orders and places further out quoted on request.

Keep it simple

A short name or web address reads best on a panel at garden distance. Custom sizes are available on request if you need a bed that fits a specific spot.

Before you plant

Branded beds are filled the same way as any other: level ground, topsoil and compost, watered well. The filling guide has the steps. To see where we deliver, read about Pretoria and Centurion.
