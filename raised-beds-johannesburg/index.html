Concrete raised beds delivered to Johannesburg

Johannesburg suburbs are not on the list of areas we regularly serve, which covers Pretoria, Centurion and Midrand among others. So before you order, WhatsApp Riaan with your suburb and he will confirm delivery. For bulk orders or places further out, he will give you a quote.

Why concrete for a Johannesburg garden

  • Strong – solid concrete panels built to take the weight of wet soil.
  • Weatherproof – sun, rain and frost don’t bother it.
  • Won’t rot or rust – no treating, no painting and no replacing.
  • Bolted panels – easy to assemble, and tidy when finished.
