Why concrete

A garden bed you only buy once

Wooden beds rot. Metal beds rust. Concrete just stays put, through sun, storms and dry winters, year after year.

  • Strong – solid concrete panels built to take the weight of wet soil.
  • Weatherproof – sun, rain and frost don’t bother it.
  • Won’t rot or rust – no treating, no painting, no replacing.
  • Bolted panels – easy to assemble and tidy when finished.
  • Great for veg, herbs and flowers – from tomatoes to marigolds.

Good to know

Questions people ask

How deep are the beds?

Every bed is 350mm high, which gives you a good depth of soil for vegetables, herbs and flowers.

How do I fill a concrete raised bed?

Put the bed on level ground, then fill it with a mix of good topsoil and compost. Water well and let it settle before you plant.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is R350 across Pretoria and Gauteng. For bulk orders or places further out, WhatsApp Riaan for a quote.

Is assembly difficult?

No. The panels are bolted together, so the beds are easy to assemble.

Can I get a custom size?

Yes. Custom sizes and bulk orders are available on request. WhatsApp Riaan with the size you need.
